Netmera is awarded the Best Marketing Automation Technology

 

June 20, 2023
 

LONDON (PRWEB)
 
JUNE 20, 2023

Netmera
, a leading Omnichannel Customer Engagement Platform designed for marketers, announced its prestigious win of the Best Marketing Automation Award by MarTech Awards. This award’s recognition underscores Netmera’s commitment to revolutionizing marketing automation and highlights the success of its partnerships with fastPay, a digital wallet company, and NeoHub, a renowned technology hub.

In the marketing technology sector,
 
Martech Awards
‘ Best Marketing Automation Award is a well-known and essential recognition. It rewards exceptional marketing automation platforms for their innovation, efficiency, and all-around excellence.

Netmera’s award-winning case study revolved around
 
fastPay
‘s struggle with incomplete transactions in their mobile application. Users were exiting the screen without completing the process due to various challenges. To address this issue, fastPay used Netmera’s push notification automation capabilities to send instructions to users facing problems through the application.

Netmera helped fastPay analyze its app users’ struggle points and conversion tendencies with in-depth Funnel Analysis. By gaining valuable insights from the data, fastPay was able to optimize its push notification strategy effectively and improve user engagement. The efficiency of communications increased by almost 80%, ensuring that users received timely and relevant instructions to resolve their transaction problems. Furthermore, the in-app CTR was boosted by 55% with personalization, indicating increased user interaction. Additionally, fastPay’s complaint rate decreased, highlighting the enhanced user experience provided through Netmera’s automated push notifications. Most notably, fastPay’s conversions doubled.

Netmera continues to drive innovation in marketing automation, empowering marketers to create personalized and engaging customer experiences across channels.
 

Through its partnerships and advanced technology, Netmera is committed to enabling brands to achieve their marketing goals, improve customer satisfaction, and drive business growth. To learn more about how Netmera helps brands to build cross-channel experiences, you can request a 
demo
