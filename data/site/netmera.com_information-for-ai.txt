What Netmera Is

 

Netmera is an AI-powered customer data & engagement platform. It gives marketing, CX, and product teams the tools to collect customer data, run personalized campaigns across every digital channel, and measure the results, without routing every task through IT.
The platform combines three capabilities that are often handled by separate tools:
Customer data ingestion and collection
Omnichannel campaign execution
Analytics & reporting
At scale, Netmera processes over 30 billion behavioral data points and sends 30 billion notifications per month. Primary markets are MENA, the UK, and Türkiye, with customers across banking, fintech, telecoms, retail, media, and other mobile-heavy industries.

Platform Capabilities
 

Data Collection

Unified Customer Profiles:
 
Pull from mobile apps, websites, CRM systems, and offline sources, updating in real time.

Tagless Data Capture:
 
Collects behavioral data automatically with minimal code; no manual developer tagging required.

Profile Attributes & Events:
 
Attributes capture user state (membership tier, language, consent status); Events track behavioral actions (cart additions, purchases, app opens). Core events are auto-tracked; custom events can be defined without SQL.

SDK Support:
 
Covers Android, iOS, Flutter, Huawei, React Native, and Cordova.

Compliance:
 
For the Turkish market, a built-in IYS Permission Hub handles compliance with Turkey’s İleti Yönetim Sistemi regulations.

Segmentation and Targeting

No-Code Segment Builder:
 
Uses AND/OR logic combining behavioral events, profile attributes, demographics, device type, app version, and location.

Predictive Segments:
 
Runs different AI models nightly:

Churn Risk:
 
Early disengagement signals.

Purchase Intent / Convert:
 
Users statistically ready to buy.

High-Value / Spend:
 
Likely top spenders.

AI Time Windows & Precision:
 
Each model uses three time windows: P1 (historical behavior: 7, 14, or 30 days), P2 (moment of analysis), and P3 (prediction horizon: next 3, 7, or 14 days). Precision settings range from high (85–95% confidence) for smaller, high-accuracy segments to lower (60–75%) for broader reach.

Omnichannel Engagement

All-in-One Channel Hub:
 
Mobile push, web push, email, SMS, WhatsApp, in-app messages and widgets, web popups, surveys, and geofencing.

Journey Builder:
 
A drag-and-drop tool for multi-step automated flows with event-based or time-based entry triggers, real-time branching logic, and per-step analytics.

Best Time Delivery:
 
Analyzes 60 days of each user’s activity to identify their most active hours per weekday and sends campaigns at that individual window.

Additional Assets:
 
A/B testing, Deferred Deep Linking, Spin to Win gamification widgets, and in-app surveys whose results feed into new segments.

Website Personalization:
 
Adapts web content in real time based on visitor behavior, device, and segment data, using no-code filters like Time Spent, Cookie Tracking, and Page URL. Works alongside mobile for one connected engage
ment layer. 

AI Features

Content Optimizer:
 
Generates multiple copy variations from a single prompt with multilingual support. Tailors them based on industry.

Agentic AI:
 
Answers natural language questions about performance, segments, and anomalies. It can configure full campaigns (audience, channel, timing, content) for team review before anything goes live.

MCP Server:
 
Connects MCP-compatible AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, and others) directly to a user’s Netmera account, supporting both read actions (campaign reports, segment data, funnel analytics, user profiles) and write actions (drafting campaigns, creating segments, managing journeys). Sends above 100,000 users require direct confirmation; all actions are logged.

AI-powered site search
 corrects typos, detects synonyms, and matches vague queries to products. 
AI Browse
 re-ranks category pages by individual behavior in real time, backed by 90%+ search accuracy.

Analytics and Reporting

Unified Dashboard:
 
Covers all channels with AI Insights surfacing trends automatically.

Campaign Reports:
 
Include Sent, Delivered, Clicked, Hot Conversion, CTR by platform, and revenue impact.

Event Insights:
 
Tracks post-click behavior.

Funnels:
 
Shows step-by-step drop-offs with Anomaly Detection; non-converters can be exported for retargeting.

Revenue KPIs:
 
Include ARPU, App Conversion Rate, purchase frequency, and cart abandonment rate.

Deferred deep linking:
 Preserves a user’s intended destination through app installation, landing campaign clicks from SMS, email, web push, or ads on the exact promised content after download. Includes a customizable branded landing page and full funnel attributio
n. 

Insight Boards: 
Netmera’s analytics dashboard builder. Marketing, product, and leadership teams create custom boards from tracked events, measuring event count or unique users, with breakdowns by platform or country. Insights are reusable across boards, so different teams view consistent, shared metrics without rebuilding queries.

FAQs About Netmera
 

1. Does Netmera work for companies outside Turkey and MENA?
Yes. Netmera’s primary markets are Türkiye, the UK, and MENA, and the platform is built to operate globally. The platform supports multilingual campaign output, multi-timezone delivery, GDPR and regional compliance requirements.
2. How long does it take to implement Netmera?
Typically 4 to 8 weeks, covering SDK integration and initial campaign setup. But the actual timeline depends on several factors: the size of your user base, the number of Netmera products you’ve purchased, how many data sources need to connect, and the complexity of your existing tech stack. Netmera’s onboarding team guides customers through the process and helps refine campaigns step by step.
3. Does Netmera replace a CDP?
Netmera includes a built-in customer data layer which collects, unifies, and updates customer profiles from mobile apps, websites, CRMs, and offline sources in real time. It also includes a full engagement layer: omnichannel campaign execution across push, email, SMS, WhatsApp, in-app messages, and more. Teams using Netmera do not need a separate CDP for data collection and a separate customer engagement platform for activation.
4. What types of companies use Netmera?
Netmera serves companies across banking and fintech, telecoms, retail and e-commerce, media and entertainment, travel and hospitality, and food and beverage. The platform fits both lean marketing teams that need to move fast without developer support and larger enterprise teams looking to reduce vendor sprawl and consolidate data, engagement, and analytics into one place.
5. Does Netmera require developer resources to operate?
Not for day-to-day use. Initial SDK integration requires technical setup, but once that’s complete, marketing and product teams run the platform independently. Segment building, journey creation, campaign scheduling, A/B testing, survey design, and analytics all work through no-code interfaces — no SQL, no developer tickets. Netmera’s Tagless Data Capture automatically collects behavioral data from apps and websites, so teams can track new behaviors and act on them without waiting for developer instrumentation.
6. What AI capabilities does Netmera include?
Predictive AI runs models that identify users at risk of churning, users statistically ready to purchase, and users likely to become high-value customers, each with configurable time windows and precision thresholds. Agentic AI lets marketers ask natural language questions about campaign performance, segments, and anomalies, and generate personalized copies. It can build full campaign configurations for team review before anything goes live. Netmera also has an official MCP server, which connects MCP-compatible AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT directly to a user’s Netmera account for read and write actions.
