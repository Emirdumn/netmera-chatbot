What are Web Push Notifications?
 

Web push notifications are messages sent to users’ devices via the website. 
Even when users are not actively browsing a website, users’ web browsers can still receive brief messages or alerts. Similar to push notifications for mobile apps, these notifications are sent by websites that users have previously subscribed to and show up in real time on users’ screens.
These notifications have the potential to be an effective tool for attracting visitors back to your website, increasing conversion rates, and engaging users. To avoid opt-outs and a bad user experience, it’s crucial to use them strategically and refrain from bombarding users with too many or irrelevant notifications.
 

Examples of Web Push Notifications
 

News Updates:
 
Delivering breaking news alerts or updates on topics of interest to users.
E-commerce Promotions:
 Notifying users about sales, discounts, or new products on an online store.
Content Updates:
 Alerting users when new blog posts, articles, or videos are published.
Abandoned Cart Reminders:
 Reminding users about items left in their shopping carts and encouraging them to complete the purchase.
Event Reminders:
 
Send reminders about upcoming webinars, events, or appointments.
