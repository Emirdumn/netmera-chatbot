The right channel
 for every financial interaction
 

Your team launches campaigns across 
push, in-app, SMS, email
, and 
WhatsApp
 from one platform. A loan reminder, a feature nudge, a retention offer, each reaching the right customer on the channel they already use to manage their money.
 

Eliminate install friction with 
Deferred Deep Linking
 

Guide users from any campaign straight to specific in-app financial products. If the app isn’t installed, Netmera directs them to the store and then automatically opens the intended loan offer or application page after download. You preserve context and prevent drop-offs.
