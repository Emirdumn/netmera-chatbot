For subscription platforms
 

Grow paid subscriptions and keep the ones you have. Netmera tracks what subscribers watch, read, and skip, and 
lets you act on
 it before they start looking for the cancel button.
 

 

From free to paid, 
with Journey
 

A free user gets a reminder nudge to upgrade. No action, and Journey switches to an in-app message comparing annual and monthly pricing. Still no conversion, and a behavior-based discount offer goes out automatically.
Journey Builder 
runs this entire sequence
 across push, in-app, SMS, and email from a single flow.
 

 

Win back subscribers 
 before they cancel
 

Give subscribers a reason to return every day.
Gamified

Automated

Personalized
 “continue watching” nudges pick up exactly where each viewer left off and watchlist reminders keep users coming back.
 

 

AI that reads your audience
