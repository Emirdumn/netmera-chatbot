Walkers
 

									Walkers is an innovative app in the tech industry that aims to provide users with a seamless, engaging experience while driving long-term retention. By collaborating with us, Walkers sought to improve daily active user numbers, boost engagement, and retain its growing user base in a competitive market.								

 

Challenge
 

Walkers faced a challenge in increasing the number of daily active users while promoting more frequent interactions. With a competitive landscape, the app needed to create a satisfying user experience that attracted new users and retained its current audience. 
Their primary goal was to keep users engaged daily and improve retention metrics.
 

Solution
 

Daily engagement through 
spin-to win
 

									We introduced the “Spin to Win” feature, a dynamic engagement tool designed to incentivize daily logins. Users were invited to spin a digital wheel each day, earning points that could be redeemed for discount coupons within the app. This gamified experience encouraged users to return to the app daily and increased the time they spent within the app, boosting overall engagement rates. The initiative directly contributed to a significant improvement in retention metrics, as users were motivated by the tangible rewards tied to their continued app usage.								

 

 

Optimized user interaction through 
data-driven messaging
 

									Leveraging our advanced analytics, we identified the most active peak hours users within the app. With this insight, we crafted targeted in-app messages reminding users to complete specific actions during these optimal times. By aligning these reminders with user behavior patterns, Walkers ensured that important actions were not overlooked, increasing click-through rates (CTR) and a higher rate of completed transactions. This strategy enhanced the user experience and aligned perfectly with Walkers’ marketing objectives, driving retention and conversion rates.								

Results
 

 

Retention rates
 

									The daily engagement driven by the “Spin to Win” feature resulted in a significant increase in retention, as users consistently returned to the app for their daily rewards. 
The spin-to-win feature increased retention rates by approximately 10.9% even after +28 days.
 

 

User engagement
 

									The engaging “Spin to Win” feature contributed to user acquisition through word-of-mouth and social sharing, generating organic growth. 
The active user count increased by approximately 11.87%.
