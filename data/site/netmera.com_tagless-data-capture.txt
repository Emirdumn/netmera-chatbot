Banking and Finance:

Smarter Onboarding Journeys

Challenge: 

A digital bank wants to increase the conversion rate of loan applications but lacks insight into where potential customers drop off during the application process.

Solution: 

With tagless data capture, the bank automatically tracks each user’s journey across the loan application flow—fields interacted with, time spent on each step, and where users exit the process.

Benefits: 

By identifying friction points, the bank can streamline the application experience, send personalized follow-up messages (e.g. “You’re almost there!”), or offer contextual support. This results in higher loan application completion rates and improved customer satisfaction.

 

E-commerce

Cart Recovery Increase

Challenge: 
An e-commerce company faces a high rate of cart abandonment and wants to reduce it.

Solution: 
The company automatically collects user behavior throughout the shopping process, including product views, time on product pages, and actions after adding items to the cart.

Benefit :
The company can identify cart abandonment points and implement strategies like targeted e-mail reminders, personalized discounts, or a streamlined checkout process to encourage purchase completion.

 

Media & Entertainment

Content Viewership Analysis

Challenge
: A streaming platform aims to enhance user engagement by understanding how viewers interact with content.

Solution
: The platform automatically captures critical user interactions like video plays, pauses, stops, and completions; showing which shows are binge-watched, where users drop off, and the types of content that lead to re-subscriptions.

Benefit
:  These insights enable the platform to tailor content, create personalized playlists, and optimize recommendations, ultimately increasing viewer retention and satisfaction.

 

Travel and Hospitality

Personalized Travel Recommendations

Challenge: 
  A travel agency offering online booking services wants to increase conversion rates by providing personalized travel recommendations.

Solution: 
By using Autotracking, the agency can track user interactions like separate search queries, screened destinations, and booking actions in details.

Benefit :
This enables targeted promotions and recommendations, such as suggesting hotels in frequently searched destinations or offering discounts on similar trips, increasing engagement and bookings.

 

Retail

Driving more sales online and in-store

Challenge: 
 A retail chain with an online presence wants to create a seamless omnichannel experience for its customers to increase conversion at its shops.

Solution: 
 The chain integrates Autotracking to automatically capture user interactions like product views, add-to-cart actions, and purchases, and sync with online and in-store data.

Benefit :
The company can offer personalized promotions, such as sending discount codes to customers who browsed a product but didn’t purchase. This results in increased customer satisfaction and higher conversion rates for both the online and in-store channels.
