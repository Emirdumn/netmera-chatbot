# Mobile In-App

Mobile In-App helps you engage users inside your mobile app with on-screen widgets. Use it for promotions, onboarding prompts, announcements, coupon reveals, and behavior-based nudges that drive taps and conversions.

### Common use cases

* Promote campaigns, launches, and limited-time offers.
* Guide users through onboarding and key next steps.
* Drive in-app actions with focused calls to action.
