Success Story
 

Location, timing, scale: how Chippin ran campaigns across three retail partners
 

~8% average open rate across geofence campaigns
 

A significant share of geofence-driven revenue came from users who had not shopped at partner stores in the previous month
 

30–60x higher revenue per message: location-triggered vs. broadcast push
 

3 retail partners, 1 coordinated two-channel strategy
 

About Chippin
 

Chippin
 is Turkey’s first mobile payment-enabled shopping and loyalty app. Users pay in-store, online, or on the go across hundreds of partner brands and earn reward points on every transaction. Retail partners span major grocery, market, and home improvement chains.
Every campaign Chippin runs is designed to build a habit: users return to partner stores, pay with Chippin, and earn more with each visit. Knowing a relevant campaign will be waiting at their favorite stores, users develop genuine trust and loyalty in the app. 
 

The challenge
 

Users at two leading supermarket chains, and a major home improvement retailer were actually engaged with Chippin: they were paying in-store, interacting with the app during visits, and showing genuine interest in partner campaigns. The behavior was there.
The team saw an opportunity to build on this foundation. Bringing more visibility to those in-store moments would create a smoother, more connected shopping experience.
 

01
 

Reaching users at the moment of purchase
 

Chippin’s campaigns required users to complete purchases across multiple visits to partner stores. Users were already inside partner stores with Chippin on their phones, but without a timely signal, the campaign moment passed unnoticed.
 

 

How Chippin solved it
 

The team set up geofence triggers across all three retail partners using Netmera. The moment a user entered a store, a message fired automatically, referencing the active campaign for that specific retailer and the reward waiting for them. Setup ran once per campaign, with Netmera handling delivery from there.
 

What changed
 

Geofence campaigns averaged an ~8% open rate across all three partners, 40 to 50 times higher than push in the same period. A meaningful share of users who converted through geofence triggers had not made a purchase at that retail partner in the previous month. Geofence brought lapsed users back into the purchase flow at the moment they were physically present in the store. 
That reactivation effect, combined with high purchase intent, explains why geofence generated 30 to 60 times more revenue per send than broadcast push, despite reaching a fraction of the total audience. 
 

02
 

Keeping campaigns visible between store visits
 

Geofence messaging covered the in-store moment, but campaigns ran over multiple days and required repeat visits. Users who hadn’t entered a partner store recently had no visibility into active campaigns or approaching deadlines. Awareness alone was a conversion lever Chippin hadn’t fully used.
 

How Chippin solved it
 

The team ran parallel push notification campaigns across all three retail partners using Netmera. Messages highlighted campaign details, reward amounts, and deadlines to pull users back to the app. Every registered user received these communications, regardless of whether they had visited a store recently.
 

 

What changed
 

Push campaigns reached millions of users across all three partners combined. Open rates sat between 0.15% and 0.2%, consistent with broad-audience push benchmarks. The channel contributed roughly 40% of total campaign revenue, a meaningful share given the scale of reach and the low per-message cost of delivery.
 

Some users hadn’t purchased in a month, but they were still walking into stores. Geofence reached them there, while push notifications kept Chippin visible for everyone else until they were ready. Both the support from Netmera’s team and their customer engagement platform made running two channels in one coordinated campaign possible.

03
 

Finding the channel that drives efficiency
 

Chippin had data on campaign performance: Push notifications reached millions while geofence reached far fewer. Without channel-level revenue data, there was no basis for deciding where to invest next.
 

How Chippin solved it
 

Netmera’s reporting gave the team a clear view of revenue per sent message across both channels. Because geofence and push ran simultaneously across the same retail partners and campaigns, the results were directly comparable, with no external factors distorting the picture.
 

What changed
 

Geofence messages reached users inside the store, at the moment of highest purchase intent, and a notable portion of those users had been inactive at that partner in the prior month. Push covered scale and kept campaigns visible between visits. Geofence drove efficiency and reactivation. Together they gave Chippin a complete picture of where to invest next.
