Banking and Finance

Onboarding

In the banking and finance sector, it is essential to implement an efficient onboarding process for new customers. When a customer opens an account, sending an automated welcome email with personalized messages and links to tutorials on key banking features—such as online banking and mobile deposits—can enhance their initial experience.

Following this, a series of push notifications can guide customers through setting up their profiles, downloading the mobile app, and exploring features that meet their financial needs. After 30 days, an automated feedback survey can help assess the onboarding experience, allowing you to refine future communications. Additionally, based on customer interactions, targeted cross-sell offers for products like credit cards or personal loans can be triggered, which can lead to increased engagement and revenue opportunities.

 

E-commerce

Abandoned Cart Recovery

In the e-commerce sector, tackling abandoned carts is essential for improving conversion rates. When a shopper adds products to their cart but fails to complete the purchase, sending an automated push notification within an hour can effectively encourage them to take action. This notification can provide a direct link to the items left in their carts, facilitating a seamless return to the checkout process.
If the customer remains inactive after 24 hours, an SMS notification offering a limited-time discount can encourage them to complete their purchase. By tracking customer interactions with these reminders, you can optimize future communications. If the purchase is still not completed, a follow-up email featuring customer reviews and suggestions for similar products can create urgency and enhance the likelihood of conversion.

 

Media and Entertainment

Content Engagement

In the media and entertainment industry, maintaining viewer engagement is vital. After a subscriber finishes watching a popular series, an automated thank-you message can acknowledge their viewership. Following this, analyzing their viewing habits to recommend similar shows and movies can improve retention by using segmentation.
Sending personalized notifications about new releases that match their preferences will keep subscribers informed and engaged. Utilizing interactive polls to gather feedback on recommended content can help adjust future suggestions based on viewer preferences. If a subscriber becomes inactive, a targeted re-engagement campaign can highlight exclusive content and upcoming shows, encouraging them to return.

 

Travel and Hospitality

Personalized Trip Planning

In the travel and hospitality sector, providing exceptional customer experiences is crucial. When a customer books a trip, a personalized welcome message can confirm their reservation and provide key destination information. As the trip approaches, automated reminders can include packing tips, local weather updates, and travel advisories.
Offering personalized recommendations for local attractions, dining options, and activities based on their preferences can significantly enhance their journey. During their trip, real-time data allows for timely push notifications featuring special offers for nearby experiences. After the trip, sending a follow-up survey to gather feedback can facilitate continuous improvement and help you tailor future offerings to meet customer needs.

 

Retail

Loyalty Program Engagement

For retail owners, fostering customer loyalty is essential for driving repeat business. Once a customer joins a loyalty program, a welcome widget about program benefits and how to earn points can set clear expectations. Regular updates about their points balance and personalized offers based on shopping history can maintain engagement.
As customers reach milestones in points accumulation, notifications about rewards and exclusive discounts can encourage continued participation. Providing personalized product suggestions based on previous purchases further enhances the customer experience. If a customer has not made a recent purchase, a tailored re-engagement email featuring special offers can effectively bring them back into the store.
