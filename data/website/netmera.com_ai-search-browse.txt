Precise results, even for imprecise searches
 

Real search behavior is unpredictable. Misspellings, synonyms, vague descriptions. Buyers don’t think in catalog taxonomy. Netmera’s AI search does, so they don’t have to.
 

Typo Tolerance

A misspelled query is still a clear signal. The engine corrects in real time and returns accurate results, so a shopper who types "keybaord" sees keyboards, not an empty page.

Synonym Detection

Two buyers searching for the same product might use completely different words. Get both to the right place, without any manual configuration.

Thematic Search

Descriptive queries are matched to the right category without exact keyword alignment. Your catalog becomes accessible to shoppers who know what they need but not what it’s called.

Alternative Suggestions

When a searched product isn’t in the catalog, surface the closest alternatives instantly. No dead end, no lost sale.

FAQs
 

 How does AI Search & Discovery handle high search volumes without slowing down?  

Netmera’s AI search delivers results in 50ms with 99.99% API uptime, so performance stays consistent regardless of traffic spikes or catalog size.
 

 How does the search engine understand user intent beyond exact keywords?  

									The NLP engine analyzes context, synonyms, and descriptive queries to match shoppers with the right product. Typo tolerance and thematic search ensure relevant results even for messy or vague queries.								

 How quickly does personalization adapt to shopper behavior? 

In real time. Every click, view, and purchase is processed instantly, adjusting product rankings and results for that specific shopper during the same session.
 

 Can my team make catalog changes without developer support? 

Yes. The no-code dashboard gives non-technical teams full control over product organization, collections, and rankings.
 

 What metrics does the analytics dashboard track?  

You get real-time data on top searches, no-result queries, conversion rates, and how shoppers interact with your search and browse experience. A/B testing results are tracked in the same dashboard.
 

 Do I need a separate integration to use AI Search & Discovery?  

No. If you are already on Netmera, AI Search & Discovery is accessible directly from your existing dashboard. No additional setup required.
