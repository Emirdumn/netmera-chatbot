Netmera’s 
AI Site Search
 & 
Discovery
 engine powers the entire product discovery layer, from search results to category pages and recommendations.
Every search query is ranked in real time based on behavioral signals, purchase intent, and affinity. Category pages dynamically reorder products for each shopper, so the most relevant items appear first without manual merchandising.
 

 

Your online and in-store data, 
in one place.
 

Purchase history from physical stores, browsing behavior and transactions from your app and website, all flow into one 
unified customer profile.
 Build segments and campaigns from the complete picture of how each customer shops with your brand.
 

 

The window is short. 
Journey
 takes the cue.
 

When a shopper abandons a cart or stalls at checkout, an automated journey starts instantly, pulling the exact product into
 a personalized recovery sequence
 across their preferred channel. The flow runs on its own. Your team stays in control of what goes live.
 

 

WhatsApp, push, email, SMS. 
Wherever your shoppers are.
 
 

Retail customers move across channels constantly. Reach them with personalized offers, order updates, and re-engagement messages on the channel that fits each moment, 
all managed from one place.
 

 

Predict who's ready to buy.
 Reach them first.
 

AI in Netmera
 identifies shoppers showing strong purchase intent
 before they find it elsewhere. 
Generate personalized copy instantly, deliver it at each user’s peak activity hour, and track exactly what converts.

eal-time campaign trigger.
 

Set 
geofences
 around your stores, malls, or competitor locations and reach customers with personalized offers the moment they cross the boundary. Every message triggers automatically based on where your customer is right now.
