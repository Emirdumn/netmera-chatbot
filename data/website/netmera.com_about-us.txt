Our Story
 

 

As one of Turkey’s first Java developers, Ahmet Başaran led large-scale projects across various industries. Everywhere he went, the story was the same. Digital marketing and product teams had the ideas and the urgency. What they didn’t have was independence.
 

In 2014, we launched the first Netmera product with a clear vision: one platform for data, engagement, and analytics. No IT dependency for every move. By 2019, Netmera Turkey spun off as an independent company, and we’re pushing that vision forward with new markets, and a product that runs complex journeys for enterprise teams across regions.
 

What We Do & Why
 

Marketing and product teams used to manage data in one tool, analytics in another, engagement in a third. Netmera ended that.
Our platform pulls customer data from mobile apps, websites, CRM, and physical channels into one place. Teams create behavior-based segments, predict actions with AI, and launch personalized campaigns across push, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app messaging. Without asking IT.
Built mobile-native, Netmera brings that same engagement power to the web: popups, push, personalization, and codeless tag management.
“We are the digital storefront for marketers on mobile and web,” says founder & CEO Ahmet Başaran. “Our customers decide what they want to promote and how they want to engage.”
When customers act, Netmera responds instantly. Real-time data triggers real-time engagement.
