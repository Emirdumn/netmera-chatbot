Frequently Asked Questions
 

 How long does Meta template approval take? 

WhatsApp messages can only be sent using templates approved by Meta. These templates are created in Meta Business Manager and automatically appear in the Netmera panel once approved. The review process usually takes around 24 hours.
 

 Can WhatsApp messages end up in spam? 

Meta assigns a 
quality score
 to each business. If users frequently mark your messages as spam, your score will drop, and your sending ability may be limited. Netmera lets you monitor this score and take proactive measures when needed.
 

 What types of content can be sent? 

Announcements and updates
Discount and campaign notifications
Personalized offers
Link-based promotions
Customer support and informational messages
Retargeting content
All of these are supported through Marketing category templates.
 

 Are CTA (button) components required in templates? 

No. CTAs are optional. However, we recommend including up to
 two CTA buttons
 to boost user interaction and conversion.
 

 How does personalization work? 

Netmera allows you to add dynamic fields (such as first name, offer details, campaign title) to your WhatsApp templates. These are automatically populated based on each user’s profile attributes—making your message feel personal and relevant.
 

 Can I send messages without opt-in? 

No. WhatsApp messages can 
only be sent to users who have explicitly opted in.
 Meta does not provide visibility into which users are opted-in; the system will only deliver messages to verified opt-in devices.
 

 How is WhatsApp used within Journeys? 

WhatsApp can be added as a step in your automated Journey flows—just like push, email, or SMS. You can trigger messages based on user behavior, attributes, or timing conditions.
 

 Is Meta’s API paid? Are there extra costs? 

Yes. Meta charges per message sent. The cost varies depending on message type and the user’s country or region.
