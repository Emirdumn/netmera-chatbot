Deferred Deep Links: The Feature That Turns App Installs Into Conversions
 

Most mobile campaigns break after installation. Users download your app but can’t find the product, offer, or screen you promoted. They bounce before they convert.
Deferred deep links fix this. They carry users from your SMS, email, push, or QR code straight to the right app screen even if they’re installing for the first time.
This guide shows you how to use them:
15+ scenarios: cart recovery, onboarding, loyalty, win-back campaigns
What happens at each stage: link entry, then automated follow-up
Message examples for every channel (SMS, email, push, in-app)
Setup in Netmera’s platform
