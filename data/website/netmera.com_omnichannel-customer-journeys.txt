Handbook

Customer Journeys From First Touch To Loyal Advocate
 

Your customers engage with what’s relevant to them at that exact moment and filter everything else without hesitation.
They crave experiences that connect across channels and adapt to how each person behaves.
Good news is you still control whether customers engage or ignore you. A platform that unifies data, campaign activation, and performance measurement gives you that control. With it, you see which behaviors predict conversion, which messages resonate, and where customers abandon their journey.
This handbook walks through scenarios your customers face. You’ll see how to simplify multichannel flows and personalize journeys without expansion of your team or technical overhead.
 

Part I:
 Collect & unify data – Convert new users and build complete customer profiles
Part II:
 Engage & monetize – Keep users active and engaged, which translates to more revenue
Part III:
 Retain & scale – Build loyalty and prevent churn
