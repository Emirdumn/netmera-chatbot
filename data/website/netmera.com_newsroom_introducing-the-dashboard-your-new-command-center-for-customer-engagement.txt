Introducing the Dashboard: Your New Command Center for Customer Engagement
 

January 20, 2026
 

We built something we think you’ll love.

It’s called 
the Dashboard
, and it’s your new home screen in Netmera.

All your engagement signals come together in one place with faster insights, a cleaner interface, and instant clarity into what’s happening across channels right now.

The Dashboard combines
 AI-powered insights
 with channel performance in one view. 
Daily Review
 Analysis flags critical issues with severity scores and explanations. 
Monthly summaries
 compare trends so you know if strategies are working. User behavior patterns appear next to campaign metrics.

When engagement suddenly shifts, you see a severity rating alongside a plain-language explanation of what happened. 
Weekly patterns
 reveal your best and worst performing days. You can finally answer whether your retention strategies are working without digging through multiple reports.

Each channel lives in its own card.
 Check mobile push delivery and opt-in health. See how web visitors are engaging. Track email subscribe/unsubscribe rates or WhatsApp template performance. 
Everything updates in real time,
 so you know what’s happening right now.

Log in, see the signal, click through and act.

The Dashboard was built because marketing, CX, and product teams told us they needed faster, more seamless ways to identify what’s working and what needs attention. Now you can see key signals the moment you log in.

The Dashboard is live now. Log in to explore.
