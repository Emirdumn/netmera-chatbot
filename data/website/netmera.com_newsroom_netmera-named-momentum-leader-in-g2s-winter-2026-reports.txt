Netmera Named Momentum Leader in G2's Winter 2026 Reports
 

Dec 15, 2025
 

AI-powered customer engagement platform recognized for rapid growth in customer adoption and satisfaction
 

Istanbul, Türkiye —
 
Netmera, an AI-powered customer engagement and growth platform, announced it has been named 
a Momentum Leader in G2’s Winter 2026 Grid Reports
. The recognition reflects accelerating customer adoption and sustained growth across mobile-first companies in banking, fintech, retail, telecommunications, and media.
G2’s Momentum Leader badge identifies platforms on high-growth trajectories based on review velocity, user satisfaction, and market momentum. The designation signals that companies are actively choosing Netmera as they consolidate fragmented marketing and engagement tools into unified platforms.
As
 
a financial services Chief Payment Services Officer shared
: “The platform is intuitive, making it easy to create and manage campaigns in real time. We especially love the segmentation features. Being able to personalize messages based on user behavior has improved our conversion rates significantly.”
An enterprise Digital Marketing Technology Solutions Associate Director explained 
what stood out in day-to-day use: “Integration is straightforward. Building cross-channel journeys, tracking results, and acting on insights is simple, and the platform performs reliably at scale.”
A small business user highlighted
 the consolidation value: “We don’t use different tools for CDMP, SMS, Push, mailing. Netmera has it all.” 
Netmera unifies customer data from mobile apps, websites, data warehouses, and CRM systems into single profiles that power personalized campaigns across push, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app messages. AI-powered predictive segments identify churn risk and purchase intent while no-code journey orchestration lets teams launch automated campaigns without IT dependency. Built-in analytics connect engagement metrics directly to revenue outcomes.
“G2’s Momentum Leader recognition comes directly from our customers’ voices on G2, and that’s what makes it meaningful,” said Burçak Kınay, CMO of Netmera. “Every review represents a team replacing fragmented tools with one platform, launching campaigns faster, and delivering real business impact. We’re grateful for their trust and focused on continuing to earn it.”

About Netmera
Netmera is an AI-powered customer engagement and growth platform serving digital marketing and product teams in mobile-first companies. The platform combines unified customer data management, predictive AI, and omnichannel engagement tools to help teams increase revenue from digital channels without expanding IT resources. 
Trusted by leading organizations in banking, fintech, telecommunications, retail, and media, Netmera enables teams to launch personalized campaigns across all channels from one system.
