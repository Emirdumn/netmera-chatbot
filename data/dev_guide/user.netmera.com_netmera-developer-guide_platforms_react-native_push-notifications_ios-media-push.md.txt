# iOS Media Push

#### Step 1: Complete iOS Media Push Steps

To enable **Media Push** in your React Native application, follow the detailed steps outlined in the **iOS Media Push** guide > [Media Push](/netmera-developer-guide/platforms/ios/new-ios-swift/push-notifications/media-push.md)&#x20;

#### Step 2: Complete iOS Carousel and Slider Push Steps

To enable **Carousel and Slider Push** in your React Native application, follow the detailed steps outlined in the **iOS Carousel and Slider Push** guide > [Media Push](/netmera-developer-guide/platforms/ios/new-ios-swift/push-notifications/media-push.md)

#### Step 3: Update your Podfile

Unlike these steps, you need to add the pods to the very top of the `Podfile` as follows.

```ruby
// For receiving Rich Media Push, you must add Netmera pods to top of your Podfile.
pod 'NetmeraNotificationServiceExtension', "4.x.x"
pod "NetmeraNotificationContentExtension", "4.x.x"
```
